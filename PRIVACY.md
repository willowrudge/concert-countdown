# Privacy Policy for Concert Countdown

_Last updated: October 1, 2026_

Concert Countdown does not collect your data or send it to the developer.

## What the extension stores
- Your event details (name, date, location, playlist URL) and the overlay's size and position are stored on your device using Chrome's storage API.
- If you connect Spotify, your Spotify access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your device.

## What the extension sends
- If you connect Spotify, the extension sends requests to Spotify (accounts.spotify.com and api.spotify.com) to sign you in and to play, pause, or skip music on your behalf. These requests go directly to Spotify and are covered by Spotify's own privacy policy.
- No data is sent to the developer or to any other third party.

## What the extension does not do
- It does not read the content, titles, or addresses of the pages you visit. It only displays the countdown overlay.
- It does not sell data, use it for advertising, or use it for anything unrelated to the extension's purpose.

## Your control
You can remove your event details with the "Change event" button, and disconnect Spotify at any time by uninstalling the extension or revoking access in your Spotify account settings.
